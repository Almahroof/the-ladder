# FC26 Four-Player Skill Ranking System: Full Scope

**Players:** Shadow, Yusuf, Jonathan, Nana Kojo
**Game:** EA Sports FC 26 (male club teams)
**Format:** In person, same console, same settings
**Goal:** Determine, over time and with statistical honesty, which player is the strongest at FC26, measuring **pure skill** (not drafting or team-picking strategy).

---

## 1. Decisions Made

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| What are we ranking? | Pure skill |
| Do draws score? | Yes |
| Fixed categories or rating-gap points? | Rating-gap points (Elo with team-rating handicap) |
| Where are games played? | In person |
| Role of the draft | Selects each player's team pool for the season; does not influence matchups or skill ranking |

## 2. Scope

### v1 (MVP)
- Match log: date, players, teams, ratings, score, extra time/penalties flag
- Tournament table (standard football table)
- Elo skill ranking with team-rating handicap
- Head-to-head matrix
- Season setup (team pools, locked ratings)

### v2
- Draft tool (team pool selection for the season)
- Fixture scheduler (random and round-robin, with swapped-team second legs)
- Optional category-points view on the same data, for comparison

### v3
- Charts: Elo over time, form, win rate by team tier
- Confidence intervals on ratings
- Season archives and carry-over of Elo across seasons
- Optional match stats (shots, possession) to detect lucky wins

### Out of scope (for now)
- Online play, disconnect handling
- Female teams or national teams
- Betting or prediction features

---

## 3. The Draft

The draft exists to fairly assign **team pools**, not to choose matchups.

- Each player drafts **10-12 teams** per season.
- Teams are grouped into tiers by their release-day overall rating (see Section 8).
- Pool limits (suggested): at most 2 teams from the top tier, so no one stacks elite sides.
- Pools are **locked** for the season.
- Fixtures are generated by the scheduler, not chosen by players.
- Each team may be used at most N times per season (suggested: 3), to prevent one team being used repeatedly.

**Optional:** keep a separate "Draft Cup" for fun that does not affect the skill ranking.

---

## 4. Match Rules

1. Same console and controllers; same game settings, difficulty, and match length for every game.
2. Team ratings are those at game release, locked for the season.
3. Players alternate sides (home/away, left/right).
4. **Two-leg fixtures with swapped teams** are recommended: Player A uses Team X and Player B uses Team Y in leg 1, then swap in leg 2. This cancels team strength completely and is the cleanest skill test.
5. A match is complete only when the final whistle blows. Rage quits are recorded as a loss for the quitting player.
6. Extra time and penalties: the match is recorded as a **draw** for Elo and table purposes (penalty winner may be noted separately for fun, but does not change the rating).
7. Disputes are settled by the match log; no retroactive edits without agreement from all four players.

---

## 5. Match Input (After Each Match)

One form, filled in once per leg, straight after the final whistle. Target: under 20 seconds.

### 5.1 Inputs entered by the players

| # | Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fixture | Pick from list | Yes | Pre-fills date, round, leg, players and teams from the scheduler. Choose "Unscheduled match" only for friendlies outside the fixture list. |
| 2 | Player A | Locked (from fixture) | Yes | The player listed first in the fixture. |
| 3 | Player B | Locked (from fixture) | Yes | The other player. |
| 4 | Player A's team | Pick from A's pool | Yes | Pre-filled by the scheduler; can only be changed to another team in A's locked pool. |
| 5 | Player B's team | Pick from B's pool | Yes | Same as above. In Leg 2 the two teams are swapped automatically. |
| 6 | Player A goals | Whole number, 0+ | Yes | Score at the **end of normal time** (90 minutes). |
| 7 | Player B goals | Whole number, 0+ | Yes | Same. |
| 8 | Went to extra time / penalties | Yes/No | Yes (default No) | If Yes, the result counts as a **draw** for both tables, whatever happened in extra time. |
| 9 | Penalty shoot-out winner | Player A / Player B | Only if 8 = Yes | Recorded for fun and stats; does not change Elo or points. |
| 10 | Rage quit / abandoned | Yes/No | Yes (default No) | If Yes, the match is recorded as a loss for the player who quit. |
| 11 | Player who quit | Player A / Player B | Only if 10 = Yes | The app records a 3–0 loss for the quitter unless they were already losing by more, in which case the actual score stands. |
| 12 | Notes | Free text | No | e.g. "controller swap at half-time". |
| 13 | Confirmed by opponent | Tap to confirm | Yes | The other player confirms the score before it is saved. Prevents disputes. |

**Optional stats (v3, off by default):** shots, shots on target and possession for each player, copied from the end-of-match screen. Used to spot lucky wins; never used in Elo.

### 5.2 Calculated by the app (never typed in)

| Field | How it is worked out |
|---|---|
| Date, session, round, leg | From the fixture |
| Team ratings | Looked up from the locked release-day ratings |
| Result for each player | Win / draw / loss from the score (or forced draw / forced loss per fields 8 and 10) |
| Table points | Win 3, draw 1, loss 0 |
| Expected score (E_A, E_B) | Section 6, Step 2 |
| Goal-margin multiplier (G) | Section 6, Step 4 |
| Elo before and after, and the change | Section 6, Step 5 |
| Team usage count | Adds one use to each team; warns when a team reaches the season cap |
| Form, streaks, clean sheets | Updated from the match log |

Before saving, the form shows a **rating impact preview**: each player's Elo before, after and the change, so both players can see the effect of the result before confirming it.

### 5.3 Checks before saving

- Both players must be different, and both teams must be in the right player's pool.
- A team cannot be used beyond its season cap (default 3).
- Leg 2 must use the Leg 1 teams swapped; the app blocks anything else.
- Goals must be whole numbers from 0 to 20.
- If "extra time / penalties" is Yes, the normal-time score must be level.
- The same fixture leg cannot be saved twice.
- Saved results are append-only. A correction is a new entry that reverses the old one, and needs all four players to approve.

### 5.4 Example entry

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Fixture | Round 10, Leg 1, Sat 17 Oct: Shadow v Yusuf |
| Shadow's team | Borussia Dortmund (81) |
| Yusuf's team | Chelsea (82) |
| Score (normal time) | Shadow 2, Yusuf 1 |
| Extra time / penalties | No |
| Rage quit | No |
| Confirmed by | Yusuf |

**What the app then works out** (using the Section 6 formulas, if Shadow is on 929 Elo and Yusuf on 991):

- Team gap for Shadow: 81 − 82 = −1, so handicap = −20 Elo; Adj_Shadow = 909
- D = 909 − 991 = −82, so E_Shadow ≈ 0.38
- 1-goal win, so G = 1.0
- Shadow: 929 + 24 × 1.0 × (1 − 0.38) ≈ **943.8** (+14.8)
- Yusuf: 991 − 14.8 ≈ **976.2** (−14.8)
- Table: Shadow +3 points, Yusuf 0

## 6. The Rating System (Elo with Team Handicap)

### Parameters (starting values, tunable)

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Starting Elo | 1000 | All players |
| K-factor | 24 | Higher = faster movement |
| Team-gap conversion | 20 Elo per 1 team-rating point | Tune using real results |
| Goal-margin multiplier cap | 1.75 | Prevents blowouts distorting ratings |

### Formulas

**Step 1: Effective rating difference**

```
Adj_A = Elo_A + 20 × (TeamRating_A − TeamRating_B)
Adj_B = Elo_B
D = Adj_A − Adj_B
```

A stronger team raises that player's effective rating, so beating a stronger team is rewarded more and winning with a stronger team is rewarded less.

**Step 2: Expected score for Player A**

```
E_A = 1 / (1 + 10^(−D / 400))
E_B = 1 − E_A
```

**Step 3: Actual score**

```
Win = 1, Draw = 0.5, Loss = 0
```

**Step 4: Goal-margin multiplier**

```
G = min(1 + 0.25 × (goal_difference − 1), 1.75)   for decisive results (goal_difference ≥ 1)
G = 1                                              for draws
```

So a 1-goal win has G = 1, a 3-goal win has G = 1.5, and anything 4+ goals is capped at 1.75.

**Step 5: Rating update**

```
New_Elo_A = Elo_A + K × G × (S_A − E_A)
New_Elo_B = Elo_B + K × G × (S_B − E_B)
```

The update is zero-sum: what A gains, B loses.

### Worked Example

Shadow (Elo 1000) plays Jonathan (Elo 1000). Shadow uses a 78-rated team and Jonathan uses an 85-rated team.

- Team gap for Shadow: 78 − 85 = −7, so handicap = −140 Elo
- Adj_Shadow = 1000 − 140 = 860; Adj_Jonathan = 1000; D = −140
- E_Shadow = 1 / (1 + 10^(140/400)) ≈ 0.31; E_Jonathan ≈ 0.69

**Result: Shadow wins 2-1** (S = 1, goal difference 1, G = 1.0)
- Shadow: 1000 + 24 × 1.0 × (1 − 0.31) = **1016.6**
- Jonathan: 1000 + 24 × 1.0 × (0 − 0.69) = **983.4**

**Alternative: they draw** (S = 0.5, G = 1.0)
- Shadow: 1000 + 24 × (0.5 − 0.31) = **1004.6** (rewarded for drawing with the weaker team)
- Jonathan: 1000 + 24 × (0.5 − 0.69) = **995.4**

**Alternative: Jonathan wins 3-0** (S = 0, G = 1.5)
- Shadow: 1000 + 24 × 1.5 × (0 − 0.309) = **988.9**
- Jonathan: 1000 + 24 × 1.5 × (1 − 0.691) = **1011.1**

> Tuning note: after 40-60 matches, check whether the 20-per-point conversion is right by seeing whether stronger teams win more often than the model predicts. Adjust the constant accordingly.

## 7. Table Design

### Table 1: Tournament Table (the fun one)

Standard football scoring: **Win = 3, Draw = 1, Loss = 0**.

| Pos | Player | P | W | D | L | GF | GA | GD | Pts | Form (last 5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Tiebreakers in order: Points, goal difference, goals for, head-to-head, Elo.

### Table 2: Skill Ranking (the real one)

| Rank | Player | Elo | Change (last 5) | Matches | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Confidence label:
- **Clear leader:** Elo lead exceeds the estimated uncertainty band
- **Too close to call:** bands overlap

### Table 3: Head-to-Head Matrix

Rows and columns are the four players; each cell shows record (W-D-L) and goal difference. This catches cycles (A beats B, B beats C, C beats A) that a single number hides.

### Additional views (v2/v3)
- Win rate by team tier
- Upset rate (wins as the lower-rated team)
- Clean sheets
- Elo over time (line chart)
- Biggest wins and streaks

## 8. Team Tiers (for draft pool limits)

Use release-day team overall ratings. Suggested split into **five tiers**:

| Tier | Description | Example rating band (set from real data) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elite | Top ~10 teams |
| 2 | Strong | Next ~15 |
| 3 | Mid-strong | Next ~20 |
| 4 | Mid | Next ~25 |
| 5 | Weaker | The rest of the male club teams |

Tiers are for pool balancing and stats only. **Rating points, not tiers, drive the Elo.** Exact bands should be set by pulling the release ratings from the game and cutting them into five groups of reasonable size.

## 9. Data Model

**Players**: `player_id`, `name`, `starting_elo`

**Seasons**: `season_id`, `name`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `k_factor`, `team_gap_conversion`, `status`

**Teams**: `team_id`, `name`, `league`, `release_overall`, `tier`

**SeasonPools**: `season_id`, `player_id`, `team_id` (locked after draft)

**Matches**:
`match_id`, `season_id`, `date`, `player_a_id`, `player_b_id`, `team_a_id`, `team_b_id`, `goals_a`, `goals_b`, `went_to_extra_time`, `pens_winner` (nullable), `abandoned`, `quit_by` (nullable), `leg_number`, `fixture_id` (nullable for unscheduled matches), `confirmed_by`, `entered_at`, `corrects_match_id` (nullable, for corrections), `notes`

**MatchStats** (v3, optional): `match_id`, `player_id`, `shots`, `shots_on_target`, `possession_pct`

**EloHistory**: `match_id`, `player_id`, `elo_before`, `elo_after`, `expected_score`, `k_used`, `margin_multiplier`

**Fixtures** (v2): `fixture_id`, `season_id`, `player_a_id`, `player_b_id`, `team_a_id`, `team_b_id`, `leg_number`, `status`

Elo is **recomputed from the match log** whenever parameters change, so the log is the single source of truth.

## 10. Sample Size and Schedule

- 4 players = **6 pairings**.
- 20 games per pairing = **120 matches in total**, or **60 per player**.
- This is enough for a season, but treat the ranking as **provisional**: detecting a genuine 60/40 edge needs roughly 100+ games per pairing.
- Elo carries over between seasons so the sample keeps growing.
- Suggested rhythm: a fixed number of fixtures per session, with a full round-robin before any pairing repeats.

---

## 11. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Luck dominates results | Confidence labels; two-leg swapped fixtures; patience |
| Players dislike a result and dispute rules | Locked rules (Section 4) and an append-only match log |
| Handicap constant is wrong | Tune from data after 40-60 matches |
| One player has a favourite team | Per-team usage cap |
| Rating drift between game updates | Ratings locked at release for the season |
| Hot-streak distortion | Cap on goal-margin multiplier |
